# Architecture decisions

An honest log of the important choices made while building Risco: the
technology, the structure of the code, the design, and the difficulties met
along the way. It records what was tried and abandoned as well as what stuck,
so future contributors understand *why* things are the way they are before
changing them.

## How to add an entry

Copy the template below, give it the next number, and fill it in when a
decision is taken (or as soon as you can after). Entries are never deleted:
if a decision is reversed, add a new entry that supersedes the old one and
update the old entry's **Status**.

```markdown
## ADR-NNN: <short title>

- **Status**: proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR-MMM
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders**: <names>

### Context and constraints
What problem did we face? What did we know at the time, and what limited our
options (platforms, time, dependencies, user expectations)?

### Options considered
1. **Option A**: short description. Pros / cons.
2. **Option B**: …

### Decision and rationale
Which option we chose, and why it beat the others.

### Trade-offs acknowledged
What we gave up or accepted as a cost.

### Outcome and lessons learned
Filled in later: did it work? What would we do differently?
```
